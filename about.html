About us

Helping you discover places worth exploring.

Travel Recommendation is a simple travel website created to showcase destination ideas and demonstrate core web development concepts. Our goal is to make travel inspiration easy to explore and understand.

Our Goal

Make travel inspiration easy to browse by grouping recommendations into beaches, temples, and countries.

What We Value

We value clear information, simple navigation, attractive presentation, teamwork, and an enjoyable browsing experience.

Team Member Guidance

Team members should keep destination information accurate, maintain clear and simple communication, and make sure all website content is easy for visitors to understand.

Before publishing changes, review the content, check all links and images, and test the website on both desktop and mobile screens.

Team Responsibilities

Team members should collaborate effectively, keep project files organized, and ensure that updates maintain the quality, accessibility, and usability of the website.

Any updates should be reviewed carefully before they are published to the live website.
